# INFORMATION SYSTEM DESIGN TRANSITION PROPOSAL

**Status:** PROPOSED  
**Ratification Status:** NOT RATIFIED

---

## 1. Current Authority Boundary

Current project authority is established through the Research Methodology phase (concluding with Decision M3).

---

## 2. Impediment to Progress

Progress is stalled because no ratified project-control decision exists to authorize the transition from the Research Methodology phase to the Information System Design phase.

---

## 3. Requirement for a Project-Control Decision

An additional project-control decision is required to provide the mandate for moving from the current research-focused phase into the information system design phase.

---

## 4. Objective of the Required Decision

The objective of the future decision is to resolve the transition between the Research Methodology phase and the Information System Design phase.

---

## 5. Scope Exclusions

The following are outside the scope of the required transition decision:

- System architecture and technical implementations.
- Database schemas and technology stacks.
- Engineering and software development.
