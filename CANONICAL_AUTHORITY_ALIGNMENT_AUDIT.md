# CANONICAL AUTHORITY ALIGNMENT AUDIT
## Project Minore

**Status:** AUDIT

---

## 1. Primary Authority
The primary authority for the project is the **Founding Definition**.

- **Primary Document**: `RECOVERED_PROJECT_MINORE_FOUNDING_DEFINITION_v0.1.md`
- **Nature**: Originating authority for all subsequent Project Minore stages. It establishes the core mission, principles, and success conditions.

---

## 2. Derived Artifacts
The following documents are derived artifacts, meaning their contents must be logically consistent with and derive from the primary authority and established project-control decisions.

- `AI_CONSTITUTION.md`: Derived governance rules for AI operation.
- `PROJECT_DOMAIN_MODEL.md`: Derived conceptual partitioning based on project-control authority.
- `CANONICAL_OBJECT_CATALOG.md`: Derived inventory of information objects based on the domain model and project decisions.
- `INFORMATION_SYSTEM_PRINCIPLES.md`: Derived constitutional rules for system design based on the founding definition and core principles.

---

## 3. Conceptual Introduction Rights

### Documents Allowed to Introduce New Concepts
- `RECOVERED_PROJECT_MINORE_FOUNDING_DEFINITION_v0.1.md`: As the originating authority, it establishes the initial core concepts.
- **Project-Control Decisions** (e.g., Decisions A1-M3): These are the only other vehicles permitted to introduce new concepts, provided they follow the `PROPOSAL → AUDIT → RATIFICATION` lifecycle.

### Documents Restricted to Reflecting Existing Authority
- `PROJECT_DOMAIN_MODEL.md`
- `CANONICAL_OBJECT_CATALOG.md`
- `INFORMATION_SYSTEM_PRINCIPLES.md`
- `AI_CONSTITUTION.md`

These documents are prohibited from introducing new concepts; they must only organize, categorize, or formally state concepts already established by the Founding Definition or ratified project-control decisions.

---

## 4. Conflict Resolution and Precedence

In the event of a disagreement between documents, the following hierarchy of precedence applies:

1. **Founding Definition**: Prevails over all other documents.
2. **Ratified Project-Control Decisions**: Prevail over derived architectural/domain artifacts.
3. **Derived Artifacts**: These are the lowest level of authority and must be updated to align with the higher tiers.

**Constitutional Rule**: If a derived artifact (e.g., `PROJECT_DOMAIN_MODEL.md`) contradicts the Founding Definition or a ratified decision, the Founding Definition or ratified decision constitutionally prevails.
